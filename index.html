Footprint-Based Sex Classification

Research

Deep learning coursework (COMP6071): binary sex classification from footprint images for forensic triage. Progressed from from-scratch MLP/CNN baselines through ten systematic, hypothesis-driven experiments to a final transfer-learning model, with Grad-CAM explainability and an extensive, honest treatment of why a 0.94+ ROC-AUC model still shouldn't be used as standalone forensic evidence. The most interesting finding: a carefully-tuned, smaller from-scratch CNN actually outperformed larger pretrained backbones like EfficientNet-B3 on the private leaderboard.
